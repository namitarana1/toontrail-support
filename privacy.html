Privacy Policy

Your stories belong to you. This policy describes how ToonTrail for iPad and Mac handles your information.

Effective November 14, 2026

Your library

ToonTrail works offline. Your profiles, books, drawings, search history and published PDFs are stored on your device.

Photos and artwork

Photos you choose are processed on your device. ToonTrail does not send your photos, faces or artwork to a server.

Sharing and backups

When you share a PDF, you choose the app or recipient. Device backups may include your library, depending on your system settings.

Ads and tracking

ToonTrail has no advertising or analytics and does not track you. Diagnostic logs stay on your device unless you choose to share them.

Managing your data

You can restore deleted books and PDFs from Trash for 30 days, or empty Trash sooner. Deleting a profile removes its local library. Copies you have shared or backed up are managed separately.

Contacting support

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you include in your message so we can respond. Please do not send profile PINs or private photos.

Privacy questions

Email prettypals@gmail.com.
