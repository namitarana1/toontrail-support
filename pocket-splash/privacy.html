Privacy Policy

Pocket Splash is an offline water-ring game for iPhone and iPad. This policy explains the app’s local data and what happens if you contact support or visit these pages.

Effective November 15, 2026

Data stored on your device

The app stores your settings, language choice, sound preferences, reef completion, best times, Flick & Float scores, and custom reefs on your device. You do not need an account or an online profile. The app does not send these gameplay details to us or sync them between devices.

Motion controls

When motion controls are enabled, the app uses accelerometer and gyroscope readings to guide rings and respond to shaking. The readings are processed on your device for gameplay; the app does not save them or send them to a server. You can disable motion controls in the game options.

Advertising, analytics and tracking

Pocket Splash has no advertising, third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. It does not ask for your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone. It does not collect gameplay data for advertising or sell it.

Managing local data

You can clear best-time and score records through the game’s options, and delete individual custom reefs by holding their cards in the main grid. Clearing records preserves settings and reef completion. Removing the app removes its local app data; device backups or restored copies are managed separately through your Apple device and backup settings.

Diagnostics and backups

The app does not include its own remote crash-reporting service. Apple may handle device diagnostics and backups according to your system settings and Apple’s policies. These services are separate from Pocket Splash’s local gameplay storage.

Contacting support

If you email prettyeditco@gmail.com, we receive your email address and the message or attachments you choose to send. We use that information to respond and troubleshoot your request. Email is handled by the email providers involved. Please do not send passwords or sensitive information. You can use the same address for questions about, or requests concerning, support correspondence.

This support website

These static pages do not add analytics, tracking scripts, forms or cookies. The site is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information, such as IP addresses, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. Opening an email link uses your chosen email service.

Changes and questions

If the app’s data practices change, this policy will be updated with a new effective date. For privacy questions, contact prettyeditco@gmail.com.
