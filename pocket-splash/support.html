ReefRinger Support

Guide floating rings through colorful reefs. Find a quick answer below, or get in touch if something does not feel right.

How do I get started?

Choose a reef, then choose Easy, Medium or Challenging. Each difficulty card shows a preview, the ring count and your best time. Tap the card to start. Use ? for that reef’s illustrated guide, including any special conditions.

How do the pump and tilt controls work?

Hold the central pump to keep the jet running. Drag the nozzle to change its direction, and use the power slider for a gentler or stronger jet. Tilt your device to guide loose rings. Turning it upside down moves rings toward the surface; returning it upright makes them sink. The phone icon recalibrates sideways tilt.

Ambient tides and currents leave rings already threaded onto poles alone. Direct pumping and turning the device upside down can still lift them.

Why did a landed ring not count?

Check the reef’s objective. Color-matching reefs require the ring and pole to match. Every Challenging reef with more than one pole also requires color matching. Ring Sizes has thin and thick targets, Three-Pole Reef needs its quota on every pole, and Stack Builder needs a complete stack to stay settled for its hold time.

How do I play Flick & Float?

Swipe a large ring from the lower-left starting area toward the upper-right pole. You can flick available rings one after another to help guide rings already in the water. A missed ring returns to the start after hitting the bottom. Clean landings, wall bank shots, combos and the final golden ring add to your score. The guide and water conditions vary by difficulty.

How do I create or edit a custom reef?

Choose Create Custom Reef in the reef grid. Set the name, rings, Normal or Large size, poles, water conditions and optional air limit. Sliders update the preview as you drag; size snaps to Normal or Large when you release it. Save to add the reef to the main grid. Hold a saved custom reef card to edit or delete it. Changing its rules resets that reef’s records and completion.

Where are my best times and saved reefs?

They stay on the device where you played. Each reef and difficulty has its own best time, saved after successful completion. Pause, Help and background time do not count. The options menu has records, including archived times from earlier rules. Records and custom reefs do not automatically sync between iPhone and iPad.

I cannot hear the sounds, or tilt feels wrong.

Check the in-game sound button, your device’s media volume, and Options → Sound effects. The master volume and each sound category can be adjusted or muted separately. For motion, check that device motion is enabled in the game options, adjust sensitivity, and use the phone icon to recalibrate.

Does the game work offline and in landscape?

Gameplay, help, languages, saved reefs and records work offline. iPhone uses portrait orientation. iPad supports portrait and landscape; landscape puts the controls along the bottom to give rings more room.

How can I report a problem?

Email prettyeditco@gmail.com with your device model, iOS or iPadOS version, app version, reef and difficulty, and the steps that led to the problem. A screenshot can help if you choose to include one. Please avoid sending passwords or other sensitive information.
