ToonTrail Support

A little help for your next big story. Find answers below or contact us at prettyeditco@gmail.com.

Start a comic

Choose your profile, then start a new comic from Home. You can choose a sample story or begin with a blank page.

Edit a tile

Double-tap or double-click a tile to open its editor. Use Help on an editing screen for a tour of its controls.

Find a PDF

Open Published in your library. Select the PDF to view it or share a copy.

Recover a deleted book

Check Trash in the profile that owned the book. Restore it within 30 days or before Trash is emptied.

Get help

If an action fails, note the screen name, the steps you took and your iPadOS or macOS version. Keep profile PINs and personal photos private.

Contact support

Email prettyeditco@gmail.com.
