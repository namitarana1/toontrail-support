How do I start a flight?

Choose a world, select Adventure or Ring challenge, choose a difficulty and plane, then tap Start flight. Hold the plane, flick upward and release to launch. A faster flick increases the initial speed; angle the flick left or right to aim. Start the gesture on the plane: tapping alone or swiping elsewhere will not launch it. The loading indicator shows preparation progress.

How do I steer and gain lift?

Tilt your device left or right, or drag the steering wheel left/right to bank and up/down to change pitch. Holding up uses the limited lift reserve; refill rings replenish it. Climbing trades speed for height, so use gentle corrections. Game options includes alternative touch controls, sensitivity, tilt calibration and sound settings.

What is free, and what does Full Access unlock?

Desktop Adventure is free at every difficulty in both game modes. One non-consumable Full Access purchase unlocks all other fifteen worlds, all difficulties and both modes. Locked worlds appear dimmed; tap one to see the unlock screen. The purchase identifier is om.prettyeditco.paperplanestogo.FullAccess. The app displays Apple’s localized price; there is no subscription.

How do I restore a purchase?

Use Game options → Restore Purchases, or Restore Purchases on the unlock screen, with the Apple Account that made the purchase. An internet connection may be needed. Flight progress stays local; restoring ownership does not transfer scores or custom planes between devices.

The price or purchase is unavailable. What should I do?

Check your connection and try again from the unlock screen. Check that purchases are allowed in your device’s Screen Time settings. If a purchase is pending approval, you can continue playing Desktop Adventure. Contact support if the problem continues. Apple handles payment and purchase authorization; Apple’s purchase support handles refund requests.

Where are my progress and chosen plane saved?

The app saves completion, stars, scores, settings, your selected plane and custom-plane measurements on this device. There is no game login or automatic progress sync between iPhone and iPad. Device backups are managed through your Apple settings. Clear score records preserves completion stars, settings and your custom plane; uninstalling removes local app data from that installation.

What happens when I hit something?

Light bumps can change your path without ending the attempt. Hard impacts use a try and resume nearby while tries remain. Easy and Medium allow four tries; Challenging allows three. After the attempts are exhausted, the scene resets. Use pause or the back button whenever you want to stop or return to the gallery.

Why is the plane on fire?

Touching a fire ring’s rim can ignite the plane. Seek a water ring to extinguish it. Different rings offer wind, lift reserve or scoring effects; the game highlights a helpful ring for your current state. The in-game guide explains the ring types and wormholes.

Can I play offline?

Scenery, music, help, language catalogs and local gameplay work offline. Buying or restoring Full Access and opening these pages use network services. Previously verified ownership can be supplied by Apple’s cached purchase information.

How do I report a problem?

Email prettyeditco@gmail.com with the app version, device model, iOS/iPadOS version, world, difficulty and what happened. You can choose to share a diagnostic log from Game options. Logs contain gameplay and performance details: review them before sending. Never send passwords or payment-card details. You may also use the public issue tracker; everything posted there is public.
