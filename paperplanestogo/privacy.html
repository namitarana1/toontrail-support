Privacy Policy

This policy describes PaperPlanesToGo for iPhone and iPad and its support website.

Effective November 24, 2026

Information stored on your device

PaperPlanesToGo stores flight completion, stars, best scores, difficulty, language, sound and control settings, your selected plane, and custom paper-plane measurements locally. It does not require a game account. These gameplay records are not automatically sent to us or synced between devices. Apple device backups may include local app data according to your backup settings.

Motion controls

When enabled, device-motion readings steer and recenter the plane. They are processed on your device, are not saved as a motion history and are not sent to our servers. You can turn off tilt steering and use touch controls instead.

Purchases

Apple processes Full Access purchases. The app requests product information and reads Apple-verified transaction and ownership information to unlock paid worlds. Restore Purchases contacts Apple. We do not receive your payment-card details. Apple handles these services under its Privacy Policy. A separate development-only Testing edition unlocks worlds without making purchase calls.

Local diagnostic logs

When local diagnostics are enabled, logs can contain timestamps, app/build information, simulated flight positions, collision events, scores and performance information. These files rotate at approximately 1 MB, with one previous file retained. They stay on your device unless you choose to share them. You can disable logging or clear stored logs in Game options; disabling logging does not delete existing files.

Advertising, analytics and permissions

The app has no advertising, tracking SDK or third-party analytics SDK. It does not ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone or photo-library access. The game does not sell or share gameplay data for advertising. Apple may process device diagnostics or store information under its policies and your system settings.

Sharing and contacting support

If you email prettyeditco@gmail.com, we receive your email address and the message or attachments you choose to send. We use that correspondence to respond to and troubleshoot your request. Email providers process messages according to their policies. Sharing a diagnostic log opens the iOS share sheet so you choose the recipient. Public GitHub issues are public; review logs and attachments before posting. Contact us at the same address about support correspondence or to request its deletion, subject to any applicable retention obligations.

Your choices and data

Clear score records removes score and ring records while preserving completion stars, settings and custom-plane measurements. Removing the app removes its local data from that installation. Manage any device backups separately in Apple settings. You can change controls, sound, language and diagnostic logging in Game options. Purchase history and refunds are managed by Apple.

This website

These static pages have no analytics scripts, advertising, tracking cookies or submission forms. They are hosted on GitHub Pages, whose infrastructure may process technical request information such as IP addresses under the GitHub Privacy Statement. Email and external links use the corresponding providers’ services.

Changes and contact

If the app’s data practices change, this policy will be updated with a new effective date. For questions about privacy or support, contact Pretty Edit Co at prettyeditco@gmail.com.
